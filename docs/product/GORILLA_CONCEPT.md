# Gorilla Uzbekistan concept

## Status

Selected direction for an independently authored Gorilla Uzbekistan product experience. The project owner confirmed the required brand and publishing permissions on 2026-07-26. The site must not imply a commissioning or operating relationship that has not been separately documented.

## Strategic premise

Build an adult-only product-discovery experience around the five local product characters, not an aggressive sports-performance advertisement.

The site must help an adult visitor:

1. understand the Uzbekistan range quickly;
2. compare flavor, sugar status, composition, and approved product facts;
3. select a suitable variant without unverified health or performance claims;
4. find a verified place or official channel to purchase;
5. contact the local company for retail, distribution, media, or partnership matters.

The primary conversion is:

```text
choose a product → start store search → open a verified retailer or map destination
```

## Original creative territory

Working creative thesis:

> FIVE STATES OF INSTINCT

Provisional Uzbek expression for keyframes:

> BESH HOLAT. BIR INSTINKT.

The five cans behave as five signals inside one visual system. Each has its own tempo, texture, accent, and soundless motion signature, while Gorilla lime acts as the connective signal.

This is not a reskin of a beverage reference. It has its own:

- product-first information architecture;
- stage-like, signal-based composition;
- Uzbek-first localization model;
- flavor comparison and store-intent funnel;
- responsive choreography;
- motion capability policy;
- compliance and provenance model.

All campaign copy, including the Uzbek expression above, remains subject to professional transcreation and item-level brand/legal review. Project-level brand and publishing permission has been confirmed.

## Product truth boundary

The local official inventory currently exposes five products:

- Original;
- Zero;
- Extra;
- Mango–Coconut;
- Lychee–Pear.

Names, formula, caffeine, sugar, ingredients, volume, nutrition, warnings, and availability must be reverified against current Uzbekistan packaging, certificates, and an approved brand sheet before they enter production content. Facts from other markets are prohibited.

Each published claim must reference:

- market and SKU;
- source ID;
- exact source evidence;
- verification date;
- verifier;
- legal approval status.

## Audience

Primary:

- adults 18–34 choosing by flavor, sugar, and product composition;
- adult followers of contemporary music, street culture, design, and nightlife;
- visitors with purchase intent;
- retail, distribution, media, and event partners.

Do not target minors or use school/student framing, juvenile gaming language, or youth-coded mechanics.

## Visual direction

### Character

Kinetic industrial editorial:

- matte black field;
- mineral white information layer;
- provisional Gorilla lime signal;
- brushed aluminum and can-print materiality;
- macro condensation, grain, halftone, scan lines, and high-speed light sweeps;
- hard editorial crops and deliberately asymmetric type;
- flavor accents derived from approved packaging, never guessed into production.

Avoid:

- generic glassmorphism;
- neon cyberpunk wallpaper;
- liquid blobs copied from beverage templates;
- permanent custom cursor dependence;
- illegible condensed body text;
- endless decorative motion;
- sports-victory symbolism.

### Color roles

Exact brand values must come from an approved brand kit. Until then, tokens are semantic and provisional:

```text
surface-ink          near-black stage
surface-paper        off-white reading surface
signal-brand         provisional Gorilla lime
signal-original      approved Original accent
signal-zero          approved Zero accent
signal-extra         approved Extra accent
signal-tropical      approved Mango–Coconut accent
signal-lychee        approved Lychee–Pear accent
text-primary         contrast-safe foreground
text-muted           contrast-safe secondary text
state-focus          non-flavor focus indicator
state-danger         validation and legal error only
```

Flavor color never carries meaning alone; names, labels, icons, and accessible states remain explicit.

### Typography

Use a two-axis system:

- licensed display face for high-impact Latin/Cyrillic headlines;
- highly readable text face for Uzbek Latin, Russian, figures, warnings, and UI.

The official site’s font files are not reusable without a license. Before choosing faces, test:

- Uzbek apostrophe forms and all Latin diacritics;
- Cyrillic;
- numerals, units, legal text, and nutrition tables;
- variable-font subset size;
- headline collision at 320 px and 200% zoom.

Do not default to Space Grotesk, Inter, or a generic “tech” pairing merely because it is convenient. Font choice is a visual-design gate, not a scaffold default.

## Compliance boundary

The Uzbekistan implementation excludes unless item-level legal review explicitly approves:

- drinking, opening, or “before/after consumption” scenes;
- athletes or people framed as successful because of the product;
- claims that the drink creates social, sporting, cognitive, or physical success;
- content directed at minors;
- invented safety, “no crash,” medical, or performance claims;
- promotions, giveaways, or fake availability.

An age confirmation can support responsible framing but does not legalize otherwise prohibited advertising. The final warning copy and its required placement must be supplied or approved by local counsel.

## Localization

- Uzbek Latin is the default and authoring source.
- Russian is a complete equal version.
- English is added only when every route and legal string is complete.
- Routes use `/uz`, `/ru`, and `/en`; query-parameter localization is prohibited.
- Locale switching preserves the current route and SKU.
- Legal and nutrition text requires professional translation and separate approval.
- Build-time checks prevent silent mixing of languages.

## Success measures

Product and business:

- product-detail reach;
- comparison completion;
- store-search start and completion;
- verified retailer/map outbound rate;
- valid B2B inquiry completion;
- locale completion success;
- zero-result rate in store search.

Experience:

- task success for choosing a product and finding a store;
- Core Web Vitals at p75;
- accessibility and reduced-motion success;
- warning visibility;
- media abort/error rate;
- form error rate.

Scroll depth and time-on-site are diagnostic only; motion can inflate them without creating value.
