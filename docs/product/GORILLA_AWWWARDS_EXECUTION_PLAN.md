## 2. User outcome

Deliver a distinctive, original Gorilla Energy Uzbekistan product experience
that reaches the typography, product scale, spatial continuity, and motion
craft expected from an Awwwards-level creative-development studio while
remaining:

- recognizably independent rather than a clone of SPYLT or the official site;
- centered on the five-product range rather than decorative interface chrome;
- authored for desktop, mobile, touch, keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion,
  forced colors, reduced data, no JavaScript, and media failure;
- Uzbek-first with natural Russian and English parity;
- honest about product facts, availability, authorship, and brand relationship;
- reproducible, testable, performance-budgeted, provenance-controlled, and
  publishable from a clean public GitHub repository;
- continuously deployable to GitHub Pages as a public **portfolio review
  build**, without claiming a completed commercial launch until the external
  legal, assistive-technology, device, and monitoring gates close.

The target is not “more effects.” The target is one directed product film in
which every section changes the state, material, space, hierarchy, or task.

## 3. Completion invariant

The experience is complete only when a visitor can remember and use one
continuous mechanism:

> One can/signal travels through five frequencies, becomes exact product
> information, and ends in a credible Tashkent action.

Every viewport must have one dominant thought, one dominant object, and at most
one primary action. Secondary controls stay at the frame perimeter. High
pressure **stage** scenes alternate with quiet **instrument** scenes so reading
is never forced to compete with motion.

## 4. Non-negotiable boundaries

### Originality and brand

- Do not copy reference code, assets, copy, section geometry, scene order,
  masks, timings, can placement, or distinctive choreography.
- Reference study may inform only abstract principles such as oversized product
  scale, one thought per viewport, and scroll-linked continuity.
- Keep the independent-concept disclaimer accurate and visible in appropriate
  legal/footer contexts; do not present the site as commissioned or operated by
  Gorilla without separate evidence.
- Use official identity and packshots only through the approved provenance
  records. Do not redraw, recolor, distort, or invent packaging.
- Exclude consumption, minors, athlete/success implication, unverified stock,
  and unapproved claims.

### Creative quality

- No generic cards-as-default composition, dashboard chrome, glassmorphism,
  generic cyberpunk wallpaper, floating shadows, random glow, infinite marquee,
  fake terminal noise, or AI-generated marketing phrasing.
- No custom cursor replacement. Fine-pointer magnetic response and bounded
  product depth are allowed without hiding or replacing the system cursor.
- No repeated reveal as the primary mechanic of adjacent chapters.
- No all-caps body/legal copy, illegible cropped text, or flavor color as the
  only state signal.

### Interaction and motion

- Native scroll and normal links remain authoritative; no smooth-scroll library
  or scroll hijacking.
- No fake loader, percentage, mandatory intro, autoplay audio, or task-blocking
  animation.
- Full, Lite, Reduced, static QA, no-JS, and media-failure states expose the
  same product truth and actions.
- Motion stops before reading. No viewport contains more than two independently
  moving focal elements.
- Every listener, observer, timeline, media object, match-media context, RAF,
  and timer has an idempotent owner and teardown.

### Engineering and release

- Static semantic HTML comes before enhancement.
- Strict TypeScript, validated content/environment boundaries, stable semantic
  IDs, immutable state updates, and no unreviewed dependencies.
- No public push may include ignored research, source media, `.tmp`, credentials,
  private deployment identifiers, stale release claims, or unapproved assets.
- Historical test reports are context, never proof for the current frozen
  source.

## 6. Product and marketing strategy

### Adult audience and jobs

The experience serves adults choosing by flavor, sugar status, verified product
information, local availability route, or business contact. It does not use
youth-coded reward mechanics, consumption imagery, athletes, or product-caused
success. The essential jobs are:

1. recognize the complete five-product range;
2. distinguish the variants without relying on color or hype;
3. inspect approved information and warnings;
4. choose and share a product state;
5. continue to a verified Find/outbound destination;
6. reach the correct contact route.

### Conversion and CTA map

```text
range recognition
  → active flavor selection
    → product detail or two-product comparison
      → Find handoff / verified outbound destination
```

- Hero primary: Explore the range; secondary: Find Gorilla.
- Reactor primary: selected product detail; secondary: Compare.
- Product truth primary: Compare or Find according to intent.
- Product detail primary: Find; secondary: Compare/adjacent product.
- Terminal primary: Contact or Find; FAQ/culture remain supporting exits.
- Every route exposes one next best action without trapping the visitor in the
  cinematic sequence.

### Success evidence

Where privacy-safe measurement is approved, observe anonymous route-level
events for flavor selection, product-detail reach, comparison completion, Find
start, verified outbound click, locale switch, and contact-intent choice. Do not
collect names, email, precise coordinates, DOM text, or full query strings. If
analytics is absent, validate the funnel through deterministic E2E task
completion and do not invent engagement or conversion claims.

### Message discipline

- Lead with flavor/range identity, not energy-performance promises.
- Let the packshot and material sell emotion; let the paper/instrument surface
  carry exact information.
- Use Tashkent as contemporary context and authorship, not a decorative theme.
- A public case study may discuss design/engineering outcomes only when backed
  by current evidence; it must not imply commercial business results.

### Five product worlds

Each world preserves the common ink/metal/paper system while adding exactly one
primary material and one primary spatial signature:

| Frequency | Material signature | Spatial signature | Non-color identity proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original | wet black aluminum and hard lime fracture | vertical axis lock/split | centered hard geometry and etched grid |

## 8. Responsive composition contract

### Desktop and wide

- Validate `1024×768`, `1366×768`, `1440×900`, and `1920×1080` as distinct
  compositions, not only scaled screenshots.
- Sticky stages begin below the computed header and always leave the current
  heading, product, and active control readable.
- Product scale is allowed to break the grid; action copy remains on it.
- Controls live on edges or compact consoles and never outscale the product.
- Wide layouts cap the content grid while allowing authored type/media bleed.
- Pointer depth is fine-pointer only, sampled through one RAF, bounded, and
  removed outside the owning scene.

### Mobile and touch

- Author at `360×800` and `390×844`; harden at `320×568`, `412×915`,
  `430×932`, `768×1024`, and `844×390` landscape.
- Use one product at a time. No five full campaign cards, raw horizontal
  scrollbar, drag-only carousel, nested scroll trap, or hover-only content.
- Keep primary controls thumb-reachable, at least `44×44 CSS px`, with at least
  `8 px` practical separation and safe-area clearance.
- Use Lite by default for coarse pointer/data saver: finite cuts, maximum
  `48 px` local travel, no pointer tilt, no autoplay video, and no long pin.
- Remove sticky/pinned choreography in short landscape; preserve content and
  actions in ordinary flow.
- Keep the full homepage near `8,000–10,000 CSS px` at `390×844`; any excess
  requires a documented scene-level reason.
- Verify Uzbek/Russian word wrapping explicitly. Never split a meaningful word
  merely to preserve a poster shape.

### Accessibility compositions

- At 200% zoom, text-spacing overrides, more contrast, forced colors, Reduced,
  and no-JS, preserve a readable single-column order and every action.
- Product selection must remain identifiable through name, structure,
  position/boundary, and selected icon/state—not color alone.
- Fixed and sticky UI must not cover focus, warnings, errors, or the browser's
  safe areas.

## 9. Motion direction and choreography map

Use only the named grammar: signal pulse, calibration sweep, shockwave,
frequency split, can orbit/lock, product-color mask, shared-can continuity,
coordinate trace, and material light pass.

### Global sequence

1. **Truth:** semantic final content is present.
2. **Signal:** one directional cue establishes cause.
3. **Material:** the can/media resolves.
4. **Label:** selected state and action become explicit.
5. **Rest:** motion stops for reading.

### Capability behavior

| Capability | Allowed | Removed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full | bounded desktop pin/scrub, authored entrance, material cuts, limited product depth, View Transition enhancement | ambient loops, task delay, global interception |
| Lite | finite intersection cuts, ≤48 px movement, poster-first media, direct controls | pointer tilt, long pins, autoplay mobile video |
| Reduced | immediate selected state, boundary/icon/text feedback, optional ≤80 ms opacity | parallax, scrub, masks travelling through space, rotation, pinning, autoplay |
| No JS/failure | fully readable static final frames and normal links | all dependency on enhancement classes |

### High-impact moments

- Signal Boot: finite calibration, never a loader.
- Hero: can constellation locks into one selected axis; scroll handoff extracts
  the pulse without scroll-jacking.
- Manifesto: one pulse transmits through the complete statement.
- Reactor: each selection cancels only its owned transition; last intent wins.
- Compare: two visible channels enter from opposing directions and settle into
  exact data; no animated counting.
- Material Film: final poster is the loading and failure state.
- Route transition: one five-rail shutter or selected-can continuity, `≤640 ms`,
  interruptible, normal navigation fallback, focus to new `h1`.
- Terminal/footer: coordinate trace becomes five rails and closes once into one
  line.

### Interaction motion

- Hover/focus: finite signal fill/underline, `160–260 ms`.
- Press: `1 px` directional pressure, `80 ms`, no layout movement.
- Product change: outgoing `160–260 ms`, incoming `260–640 ms`.
- First meaningful entrance: `≤880 ms`.
- Exit is shorter than entry. Decorative repeat count is zero.
- Touch gets immediate semantic feedback before the visual transition.

### Optional advanced media

- Baseline sound is silent. Optional sound requires an explicit user gesture,
  original/licensed provenance, persistent mute/stop, no information dependency,
  and separate performance/legal approval.
- WebGL is not a baseline quality requirement. An isolated post-LCP experiment
  may proceed only if it produces a clearly unique product-material result and
  proves a static fallback, DPR cap, visibility pause, context recovery,
  disposal, bounded memory, device soak, and no budget regression. Failure of
  any gate removes the experiment rather than weakening the gate.

## 10. Content, copy, claims, and localization

### Content hierarchy

1. consumer-facing emotional line;
2. one neutral range/flavor explanation;
3. exact product information and warning;
4. one real task action;
5. compact provenance/concept context only where appropriate.

Do not expose internal design vocabulary such as “award-winning,” “frequency
architecture,” or provenance status as consumer headline copy. The experience
may use signal language, but it must sound like a brand experience rather than
a case-study annotation.

### Localization

- Uzbek Latin is the authored source and line-breaking authority.
- Russian and English are transcreated for meaning and rhythm, not forced into
  identical line counts.
- All enabled routes, metadata, alt text, controls, errors, warnings, product
  facts, and live-region messages require parity.
- Test Uzbek apostrophe variants, Cyrillic, numerals, `%`, `mg`, `ml`, and every
  product name with the shipped fonts and fallbacks.

### Product truth

- Every displayed fact resolves to an Uzbekistan-specific source record with a
  date and approval state.
- Unverified values are omitted or explicitly read “Not yet verified.”
- Availability language is honest and outbound destinations are verified.
- No content patch may invent a retailer, contact, warning, nutrition value,
  campaign claim, or legal interpretation.

### Layer boundaries

```text
pages/layouts
  → feature compositions
    → primitives + validated content + public motion contracts

content/config
  ↛ UI or feature internals

motion runtime
  → scene registry and public setup contracts
  ↛ feature-internal state

feature A
  ↛ feature B internals
```

- Pages remain thin route composition and metadata boundaries.
- Content, product types, UI, motion setup, and infrastructure/side effects are
  separate.
- URL owns shareable locale/product/compare state; ephemeral state stays local.
- A feature receives a local root and typed data. Production scene code never
  queries the global document for feature elements.
- The root runtime owns route lifecycle, preference controls, menu coordination,
  and one bounded refresh after fonts/LCP media settle.
- A scene cleans up only resources it owns; global teardown is prohibited.

### Feature shape

Use a file only when it has one cohesive responsibility:

```text
feature-name/
├── FeatureName.astro              # semantic composition
├── feature-name.css               # base composition
├── feature-name-responsive.css    # only when independently substantial
├── feature-name-contract.ts       # public types/invariants
├── feature-name-controller.ts     # local interaction/side effects
├── feature-name-session.ts        # state machine only when needed
└── feature-name.spec.ts           # behavior/edge cases
```

Keep modules focused, generally below 200 logical lines and functions below 30
logical lines. Split by responsibility, not arbitrary line count. Remove dead
legacy CSS/controllers after replacement; do not retain parallel old/new
implementations.

### Base-aware public routing

The public project Pages deployment requires a single validated base-path
contract. Add and document a base value (for example
`/gorilla-five-signals-concept`) and route every internal page, asset, font,
manifest, canonical, hreflang, sitemap, redirect, media source, and share URL
through one tested helper/config boundary. Do not scatter repository-name
strings through components.

The build must support:

- local/root development;
- project Pages under the configured base;
- a future custom root domain without source rewrites.

## 17. Five-round critique protocol

Every material implementation task produces a short review record with five
observable conclusions. This record contains findings, corrections, and proof;
it does not expose private internal reasoning or claim metaphysical perfection.

1. **Correctness:** edge cases, rapid intent, nullable states, failure, and
   cleanup.
2. **Architecture:** ownership, import boundaries, duplication, types, and
   maintainability.
3. **Experience:** art direction, task clarity, desktop/mobile, keyboard/touch,
   accessibility, and Reduced.
4. **Runtime:** performance, media, security, resilience, dependency, and
   hosting behavior.
5. **Release:** copy/claims, localization, provenance, documentation, evidence,
   and public wording.

A failed round requires a code/design/content correction or a measured,
documented exception. Additional rounds continue while a material weakness
remains.
