# Five Frequencies — redesign execution plan

## Status

This plan is the current visual and motion execution authority for the private
Gorilla Uzbekistan concept. It refines the accepted product, legal,
accessibility, architecture, and performance boundaries in
`GORILLA_MASTER_PLAN.md`; it does not weaken or replace those gates.

The redesign exists because the August implementation proved technically
strong but visually uneven: the hero and manifesto established an awards-level
promise while the remaining journey often fell back to repeated headings,
cards, rails, and entrance reveals.

## User outcome

Create an original, culturally grounded Gorilla Energy Uzbekistan experience
that feels authored from first frame to final action on desktop and mobile.
The result must communicate product choice clearly, remain useful without
motion, and never trade accessibility or performance for spectacle.

## Creative platform

### GORILLA: FIVE FREQUENCIES — TASHKENT AFTERDARK

The site behaves like a live transmission from contemporary Tashkent. One
signal travels through the experience; the five products tune that signal into
five distinct visual frequencies. The idea is industrial and editorial, not
cyberpunk, folklore, gaming UI, or a copy of another beverage site.

### Memory device

The visitor should remember one continuous object: a can/signal that changes
material, trajectory, typography, and spatial pressure as the selected flavor
changes. Pages and sections must not feel like unrelated campaign posters.

### Three visual voices

1. **Impact:** condensed display type for a small number of decisive poster
   frames.
2. **Signal:** data/mono language for frequency, coordinates, progress, and
   controls.
3. **Reading:** calm text face for product clarity, warnings, and actions.

Display type is not the default for every heading. Quiet reading frames are
required between high-pressure scenes.

## Homepage sequence

The homepage is limited to seven authored chapters. Two consecutive chapters
may not reuse the same composition or motion mechanic.

### 00 — Signal Boot

- Responsible entry is immediate and semantic.
- A finite scan and calibration response may confirm entry.
- No fake percentage, countdown, mandatory logo film, or blocked action.
- Reduced motion shows the final state immediately.

### 01 — Signal Captured

- Preserve the strongest five-can hero composition.
- Entrance establishes depth, hierarchy, and five-frequency range.
- Scroll separates the collage into the manifesto without smooth-scroll or
  scroll-jacking.
- Pointer depth is optional, bounded, and fine-pointer only.

### 02 — Transmission Manifesto

- Preserve the current large type direction.
- Motion transfers one pulse between phrases instead of revealing every line
  independently.
- The final pulse becomes the input to the Flavor Reactor.

### 03 — Flavor Reactor

- Desktop uses one pinned full-screen product stage.
- Only one flavor world is dominant at a time.
- Previous, next, and direct `01—05` controls remain visible and keyboard
  operable.
- Selection updates semantic content and shareable URL state.
- Mobile uses one compact, one-at-a-time stage with explicit controls and a
  visible swipe/snap affordance; it must not stack five full campaign cards.
- Gesture is never the only input.

Flavor signatures:

| Frequency     | Material                                         | Spatial behavior                                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Original      | wet black aluminum, lime fracture, night asphalt | vertical lock and hard electrical split           |
| Zero          | silver metal, condensation, cold scan            | negative-space wipe and precise lateral frequency |
| Extra         | petrol metal, bronze flare, heat trace           | compressed overdrive pulse and forward pressure   |
| Mango–Coconut | cyan/orange vapor and fiber                      | warm/cold diagonal collision                      |
| Lychee–Pear   | magenta/lime droplets and refraction             | prismatic scan and soft-edged lens split          |

These are art-direction signatures, not product-effect claims.

### 04 — Product Truth / Signal Duel

- One compact chapter converts energy into verified choice.
- Responsible information stays readable and visually secondary to the main
  emotional arc, never hidden.
- The comparison invitation visualizes a two-frequency collision rather than a
  static five-card catalog.
- Only approved product fields may be shown.

### 05 — Material Film

- The film is a sensory material beat, not another slideshow of five cans.
- Required subject language: aluminum, print, condensation, ice, controlled
  light, Tashkent night surface, and signal color.
- Mobile remains poster-first with explicit play.
- Desktop media loads only at proximity and pauses offscreen.
- A designed static poster must remain a complete composition.

### 06 — Tashkent Terminal

- Culture, find, FAQ, and contact become one decisive terminal rather than four
  repeated full-height marketing sections.
- A coordinate trace connects cultural context to the honest locator handoff.
- FAQ preview is compact; the full field manual remains on `/faq`.
- Contact is the final dominant action.

## Internal route contracts

- **Products:** spatial atlas/lab; not five repeated homepage cards.
- **Product detail:** a flavor-specific opening scene, verified information,
  and continuity from the selected can.
- **Compare:** kinetic two-frequency duel with accessible native selection and
  verified dimensions only.
- **Culture:** contemporary Tashkent signal atlas; no souvenir or decorative
  folklore treatment.
- **Find:** honest search handoff/terminal; never invent retailer availability.
- **FAQ:** compact field manual with index, grouped questions, and fast keyboard
  navigation.
- **Contact:** editorial switchboard with real destinations and no fake form.

## Motion grammar

Production choreography uses a small named vocabulary:

- signal pulse;
- calibration sweep;
- shockwave;
- frequency split;
- can orbit/lock;
- product-color mask;
- shared-element can continuity;
- coordinate trace;
- material light pass.

Motion must change state, space, hierarchy, or material. A generic reveal does
not qualify as a scene concept. Every scene owns its timeline, listeners,
observers, and teardown. All animations are interruptible; native scrolling and
normal links remain authoritative.

## Mobile direction

- Mobile is separately composed at 360×800 and 390×844, then verified at the
  complete matrix in `AGENTS.md`.
- Target homepage length is approximately 8,000–10,000 CSS pixels at 390×844;
  exceeding it requires a scene-by-scene content reason.
- No raw horizontal scrollbar, clipped product name, orphaned word fragment,
  drag-only interaction, nested scroll trap, or hover dependency.
- Product navigation is thumb-reachable and at least 44×44 CSS pixels.
- Phone landscape preserves actions and removes non-essential spatial motion.

## Asset quality

- Oversized packshots require source quality that remains sharp at the rendered
  size; CSS enlargement is not accepted as an asset solution.
- New CGI, photography, film, texture, and audio require provenance before
  release.
- A visual effect may not impersonate an official product claim or alter
  packaging artwork.
- Missing premium media must degrade to an intentionally art-directed still,
  not a placeholder.

## Brand voice

- Emotional copy must sound like a consumer brand, not a design case study.
- Provenance, approval status, and concept disclaimers remain available but do
  not dominate the emotional headline layer.
- Uzbek is written as primary authored copy, with Russian and English adapted
  for natural rhythm rather than forced line parity.
- Every new claim passes the existing truth table.

## Implementation workstreams

1. Flavor Reactor and its responsive state/motion system.
2. Signal Boot and route-transition continuity.
3. Homepage truth/duel, material film, and Tashkent Terminal consolidation.
4. Products/Compare/FAQ route art-direction uplift.
5. Five product-detail flavor openings.
6. Copy and typography correction across all locales.
7. Asset-production replacement pass.
8. Visual, accessibility, performance, and release evidence refresh.

## Acceptance criteria

- The first three minutes contain multiple distinct spatial transformations,
  not a sequence of entrance animations.
- No homepage mechanic is repeated as the primary idea of the next chapter.
- Each flavor is identifiable by composition and motion without relying only on
  color.
- The mobile homepage does not stack five full product campaign cards.
- Every interactive state works with mouse, keyboard, and touch.
- Full, Lite, Reduced, and no-JavaScript states expose the same content and
  real actions.
- All mandatory responsive viewports are visually inspected.
- Text remains readable at 200% zoom and with text-spacing overrides.
- Lighthouse production median of three meets the repository budgets.
- Long animation work remains inside the measured performance gate.
- Format, lint, typecheck, unit, integration, E2E, accessibility, visual,
  performance, build, provenance, dependency, and Sites verifiers pass on the
  final frozen source.
- Current evidence replaces historical green claims; stale screenshots and
  reports are never used as release proof.

## Five-lens review record

Every workstream is accepted only after five separate passes:

1. correctness, edge cases, rapid intent changes, and cleanup;
2. architecture, ownership, duplication, and maintainability;
3. art direction, UX, responsive composition, accessibility, and reduced
   motion;
4. runtime performance, media policy, security, and dependency risk;
5. product truth, copy quality, documentation, provenance, and release
   evidence.

Each failed pass requires an implementation change or an explicit, measured,
documented exception. “Looks good” is not evidence.

## Completion boundary

The redesign is not complete when the homepage alone looks strong. Completion
requires consistent route quality, five distinct product worlds, intentional
mobile choreography, current quality evidence, and closure of every applicable
gate above. Manual legal, physical-device, and assistive-technology approvals
remain explicit external gates and may not be fabricated.
